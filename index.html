How to Play

1
Read the order.
Customers only say the dish name. Check the menu board for its ingredients!
2
Build the dish.
Click the ingredients (or press the key on each button). Any order is fine, but no extras!
3
Serve it.
Click the customer (or press Q W E for seat 1, 2, 3).
4
Be quick!
The patience bar shrinks. Faster service = bigger tips.
  • "NO ___!" means leave that ingredient out. "ADD ___!" means add that extra.
  • Serve the wrong dish and you lose $5. Not sure? Hit 🗑 Trash (or X) and start over.
  • 3 angry customers and the level is over. Fewer angry customers = more ⭐.
  • Pause with P, music on/off with M.
